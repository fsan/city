# Bus operators and service agreements — first increment

Acceptance: the mayor can inspect three operators, publish a funded offer for a line with a duration, fleet and maximum payment, see a resource/profitability refusal or acceptance, and observe payments earned by delivered bus time. Protected reserves must prevent double spending. Expiry or withdrawal releases unused money. An operator change must preserve riders and the route.

Initial model: three authored operators with limited available buses and drivers. Price must cover £0.18 per bus-second plus a 15% margin. No speculative fare income is used to justify acceptance. Delivery counts movement and scheduled dwell by current-route buses, not stopped traffic or failed service. Settlement is in whole pennies against the existing ledger. Routes without an agreement retain the existing privately funded operation. Fleet expansion, payroll, tender competition and measured headway compliance remain later increments.
