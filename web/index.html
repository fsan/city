Transport authority

06 / MOBILITY

Fares & support

Operators charge up to £3 within the cap. Subsidies come from uncommitted city funds. Operators share company working capital across their lines; buses cost £0.18 per simulation second plus prepaid clearance. Service stops when cash runs out. Capacity: 24 seats; two buses initially; service agreements can require one to three.

Bus lines

Bus operators & service agreements

Abstract driver cohorts: day 06:00–22:00, night 22:00–06:00. Operation costs £0.06 vehicle + £0.12 labour per bus-second; dispatch prepays £2 for eventual safe clearance and relief overtime. No advances: future public payments are earned through service. Compare fewer buses, daytime hours or another solvent operator if refused.

Funds are reserved on offer. Payment follows movement and scheduled dwell; traffic delays reduce delivery. Offers need operating cost plus 15% margin.

Fleet & staff investment

Buses wear only while a service bus is moving, dwelling or held in traffic; clearing earns nothing and causes no wear. An exhausted bus stops at the next stop, unloads and enters paid maintenance. A repair that cannot be funded leaves the unit unavailable instead of creating debt. Purchases, sales, recruitment, severance and maintenance are explicit cash movements inside the operator's own account.

Agreement regularity review

Optional target: whole 30–600 simulation seconds between arrivals. Review only; no penalty or price change, and financial eligibility does not guarantee this interval is achievable. Only arrivals after acceptance count. Each coverage window allows one target-length grace period for its first arrival. Closed-hour pairs are omitted. Missing samples never mean success. The enforceable remedy above uses delivered bus-seconds against the windowed target, never these intervals.

Enforceable remedy: delivery below 85% of the agreed windowed target after at least one whole day of expected service opens a 480-second cure; an unresolved shortfall then accrues a service credit at £0.18 per missing bus-second, capped at 25% of the agreement price. Credits are paid only from operator cash above the £2.18 dispatch floor; unpaid credit is recorded as waived, never as debt. A route or service change suspends enforcement for that agreement. Regularity intervals above remain review-only and never move money.

Other remedies: inspect held buses and street allocation, edit the route, or cancel and re-offer. Cancellation pays earned pennies and releases the remainder; service continues privately. Route edits do not restart the agreement clock. Private services retain the last operator and hours. Cancel and re-offer to agree terms for the new route.

Ordered street stops · circular service

    Two to sixteen unique stops. Closing the window keeps map editing active. Drag a gold stop to relocate it; drag a connecting route segment to insert a stop. Changes unload riders at the next junction before buses join the revised service.

    Street allocation

    Bus lanes bypass general traffic queues. Cycle lanes improve cycling speed. Both allocations reduce car speed by 20%. Works, slopes and worn surfaces also slow traffic. G toggles the traffic map; click a street there to inspect it.

    Parking and street types

    Bicycle parks and car parks are seeded where districts are busy; every space has a hard slot count. Cyclists and drivers aim for the nearest place they expect to be free, fall back to the closest free space, and walk the rest of the way. Streets and avenues sell kerbside car spaces at a price banded by the movement actually observed on that segment, capped at £1.20. Lanes never allow kerbside parking.

    Traffic signals and crosswalks

    Arm a tool, then click the map. Placement snaps to the nearest junction arm. A light is set back from the corner and gives one branch green at a time, as real life does.

    Blinking yellow

    In the window below this light blinks yellow instead of cycling, which means cars may cross slowly as long as it is safe. The switch puts one light on flashing yellow by hand, or hands it back to the window and the normal cycle.

    Emergency hold

    Police and firefighters are not simulated yet. These raise the alert a dispatcher will one day send: hold stops the cross traffic so an emergency vehicle can pass, open puts the junction on flashing yellow, release hands it back.

    Apply to many lights

    Choose what to change, then whether it lands on every light, on every light along one street, or on one junction. The count after Apply says how many lights took the new value.

    Linked lights run the same cycle, with the follower held back by the delay shown on the link. Pick a leader and a follower, set the delay in simulation seconds, then Link them.

    Emergency alerts

    A police or firefighter dispatcher will one day raise these. Hold stops the cross traffic so an emergency vehicle can pass; Open puts the junction on flashing amber; Release hands it back to its normal cycle.
